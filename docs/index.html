RadioWVR Library Manager

Your sounds on this computer on the left, the WVR on the right. Drag sounds across, or copy and paste them, to put them in the WVR's slots or to keep them in your library. The WVR keeps every change as you make it; the library folder is only written when you save it.

This is the demo, not a WVR. The WVR pane on the right is a pretend one: its sounds play on this computer and are kept in this browser only. This page can't reach a real WVR.

To work on a WVR: join its WiFi network (RadioWVR-…, password 12345678), then open its own page at http://192.168.4.1. It is this same manager, served by the WVR.

    Sounds going to the WVR:

      Keys and tips

      Select: click a row; SHIFT + click a range; ⌘ (Ctrl) + click to add or remove one; click a category's name for all of it; ⌘A for everything in the pane you clicked last.

      Edit: ⌘C copy · ⌘X cut · ⌘V paste · ⌘D duplicate · ⌫ delete · ⏎ or double-click rename · ⌘Z undo · SHIFT ⌘Z redo · SPACE play / stop · Esc stops everything on the WVR · ⌥↑ ⌥↓ move slots.

      Between the panes: drag sounds from the library onto a slot, or copy them and paste with a slot selected: they go into that slot and the ones after it. To WVR → puts them in the first empty slots instead. Dragging slots onto the library, or ← To library, copies their sounds into it as WAV files.

      In the WVR: ▶ on a row plays that slot on the WVR; the selected slot's settings are beside the list and are heard as you change them. Drag a slot onto another to swap the two (hold ⌥ to copy). A slot's MIDI note and pin stay with its number: moving a sound to another slot gives it that slot's triggers.

      The library is a folder on this computer; its folders are the categories. Move to… puts sounds in another category (a new one makes a new folder; Doors/Wood is a folder in a folder). Nothing in the folder changes until you Save. Chrome and Edge save straight into the folder when the page comes from the internet; on the WVR's own page, and in other browsers, Save downloads the library as a zip to unzip in its place.

      Files: WAV and AIFF in any browser; MP3, FLAC, Ogg and others where the browser can read them. The WVR gets them as 16-bit WAV at 44.1 kHz, converted here.
